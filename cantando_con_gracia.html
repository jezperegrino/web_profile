Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes, all hymns and chords are stored locally on your device for offline use.

Do I need to create an account to use the app?

No, "Cantando con Gracia" does not require registration or any personal data. Your favorite lists are stored locally on your device to ensure maximum privacy.

Where do the lyrics and chords come from?

All lyrics are direct adaptations of biblical passages from the Reina Valera 1960 (RVR1960). We create the musical chords to accompany these scriptures, ensuring that all content is rooted in the Word of God and free for the community to use in worship.

Can I adjust the text size for better readability?

Yes, the app is designed to be accessible. It follows your device's system font settings and provides a clean, distraction-free interface for easy reading during service.
